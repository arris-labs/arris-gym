## Goal

arris-gym pins `arris==0.11.1`. `docs/ARRIS_API.md` is regenerated from its
stubs, and the gate passes on it. Every committed table that records the
kernel is re-run on it and its numbers are explained: C1's fidelity table and
arris report, C1's re-scored table (its digest equal on x86-64 and aarch64),
and C2's BenchCAD and CAD-Recode tables. Every row that moved has a cause.
Every strict `xfail` that 0.11.1 lifts is lifted, and every one it does not
lift names 0.11.1 in its reason. No `cq`, `reward` or `sandbox` behaviour
changes beyond what the kernel itself changes.

## Non-goals

- Using `surface_properties` on a `Face`: ADR-0017's exact face centre of
  mass, its margin, `workplane()` on a curved face and on a near tie, and the
  6 `connecting_rod` rows (`docs/BACKLOG.md`, the face-properties line). This
  is the next plan, `face-centre-of-mass`, planned after this one is retired.
- The cylinder side that arris splits at a box's window
  (`regression/cq-workplane-on-a-cylinder-face-split-at-its-seam`).
- Any `cq` lift, any `RewardConfig` change, and a `numpy` bump.
- Editing an ADR's text about 0.10.0: ADRs are append-only.

## Design deltas

- **The pin.** `pyproject.toml` and `uv.lock` move to `arris==0.11.1`.
  `docs/ARRIS_API.md` is regenerated by `tools/gen_arris_api.py`; its only
  expected change is `surface_properties(self, entity: Body | Face, …)`.
  No call in `src/`, `tests/` or `tools/` uses the `body=` keyword (grepped
  2026-11-15), so arris's breaking rename needs no code change here.
- **What 0.11.1 can move** (0.11.0's CHANGELOG entries; 0.11.1 is the same
  code, the first on PyPI):
  - the face quadrature, which gives a body the same figure as before only
    up to rounding;
  - NURBS-against-analytic `Coincident` verdicts;
  - the tolerances of a body moved by `transform` or `mirror` after it was
    read from a file;
  - a long, nearly tangent fillet far from the origin that ended in an
    internal error now builds.

  Each moved row is traced to one of these. Any other cause is a finding:
  it becomes a fixture, and arris is asked about it through
  `../arris/docs/ideas/`.
- **The reward's version is unchanged by a kernel bump (ADR-0021, step 2).**
  The reward's version names the meaning of `RewardConfig` and the formula.
  The kernel is named by `versions.arris`, which every row records and
  `Score.digest()` hashes (ADR-0011's amendment). So a bump moves every
  digest through that string, and only `versions.arris` says the kernel
  moved. `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` §Reward's sentence "a change that can move a
  reward bumps it" gets a clause for this. gym.md's rule on the reward's
  version gets the same clause.
- **Strings.** The `Versions(arris="0.10.0", …)` literals in tests and
  doctests are inputs, not pins, and stay as they are. Fixture texts that
  record a run (`dump/`, the reports, the README figures) are regenerated
  with the tables they belong to.

## Acceptance

- The gate passes on `arris==0.11.1`, and `docs/ARRIS_API.md` equals the
  wheel's stubs.
- `regression/`: no strict `xfail` names 0.10.0, and each fixture lifted in
  step 1 passes without one.
- C1: `fidelity.json` and `rescore.json` are re-run. The rescore digest is
  equal at 2 and 16 workers and on both CI architectures. The rescore diff
  is `versions` alone, or each moved row is explained.
- C2: on BenchCAD and CAD-Recode, in-subset `cq_bug` is at most 6 and 0
  respectively, and in-subset unclassified is 0. Reach is ≥ 9,998 and ≥ 104,
  and each reach or class change is traced to a 0.11.1 entry. Oracle rows
  are unchanged.
